Jarvis Assistant

Jarvis is a personal assistant built by its owner for personal use only. It runs on a self-hosted automation server and helps with:

  • Sending, drafting and reading the owner’s own Gmail
  • Reading and creating events on the owner’s own Google Calendar
  • Weather, news, a to-do list and Spotify playback

Jarvis is not offered to the public and has no other users.
